# 1.6 — 2026-10-29

## Added

- **Quick reply.** Answer from a box under the message, without opening the composer.
- **Saved AI prompts.** A menu of prompts for AI Writer and the assistant.
- **Contacts.** Click a sender to open the contact and give it a name on this Mac.
- **Avatars.** Gravatar when the sender has one, otherwise their initial on a color picked from their address.
- Replies sent from the reply box show under the message with sender, recipients, time and text.
- Wide emails (tables, large images) are zoomed to fit the reader instead of scrolling sideways.

## Improved

- Message list: unread mail is bold with a blue dot, read mail is lighter, and rows follow the avatar setting.
- Right-click and sidebar menus.
