# 1.2 — 2026-10-22

## Added

- **AI assistant.** A chat panel (sparkles button in the title bar) that finds mail, summarizes threads, drafts replies and tidies the inbox.
- **AI Writer.** Describe an email in the composer and the AI writes it. The result can be undone.
- **AI providers.** OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter, Groq, Ollama or a similar service. Keys are stored in the macOS Keychain. Pick a model for the assistant and another for AI Writer.
- **Inbox views.** Named views that combine several accounts, plus unified Today, Drafts, Sent and Starred.
- **Account profiles.** Per-account names and avatars.
- **License manager** and a larger Settings window.

## Improved

- Sender avatars are cached.
- Message previews are cleaner.
